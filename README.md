# vibecode-agent-skills

Constellation Academy's Claude Code skills. Right now it gives you one thing: a **security check** for your app.

Before you put an app online, it checks for security problems and gives you a clear answer: **safe to launch**, or **not yet — and here's what to fix**. It talks in plain language. No tech background needed.

---

## How to use it

Good news: **there's nothing to install.** Your Constellation Academy account already has it.

Open your project in Claude and type:

> **check my app**

That's the whole thing. Claude looks through your app and tells you whether it's safe to go live — or exactly what to fix first. (You can also say it in German: **"kann ich das veröffentlichen?"**)

When Claude asks permission to run the check, click **Allow**. That's normal and needed.

---

## Don't see it?

If typing **check my app** doesn't do anything, close Claude, open it again, and try once more. It updates itself quietly in the background.

Still nothing? Message **Sascha** — something on the account side needs a look.
